# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in DQOPR NetPulse welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about risks and bugs.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and project goals.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Pressuring maintainers or users to weaken privacy or security protections.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, discussions, pull requests, release spaces, and other project-managed community venues.
